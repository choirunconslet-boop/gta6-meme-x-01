Leonida Meme Lab — a client-side GTA VI meme generator. Pick a template, write your caption, and the app renders a share-ready meme on canvas, with make.fun's ghost mascot standing by the roadside in Vice City. No backend, no keys, everything runs in your browser.

The featured meme: a Vice City sunset highway, a `WELCOME TO LEONIDA` road sign, and the punchline — population: everyone who called in sick today. It is the entry's post on X for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty (see `assets/meme.png`).

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000
```

Or just open `index.html` in a browser. Edit the captions, switch templates, hit **Download PNG** and post it.
